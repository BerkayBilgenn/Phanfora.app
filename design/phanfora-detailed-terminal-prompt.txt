Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a visually exceptional, highly detailed desktop crypto intelligence dashboard for PHANFORA. The user explicitly wants a MODERN, RICH, SOPHISTICATED interface, not a sparse or plain minimalist screen. It should have the depth and precision of an advanced professional trading workstation and the visual design quality of a bespoke award-winning digital product. Output ONE flat front-facing 16:10 desktop screen, edge-to-edge app UI, as high resolution as possible, ideally 2560x1600. No device frame, perspective, browser chrome, external annotations or surrounding presentation board.

ART DIRECTION:
An editorially precise modern dark terminal. Graphite black background #0E1115, raised charcoal panels #171C22, barely lighter headers #1D232A, very thin structured borders #2B343C. Crisp white primary type, sophisticated cool grey secondary type. Beautiful emerald and muted coral financial chart data; a restrained electric yellow-green #DCF479 accent for selected states and a small primary button; optional very fine icy-blue moving average line. No neon glow. No marketing slogan. No enormous headings. No serif display type. No abstract radar, orb, 3D coin, neon mesh or decorative scene. No excessive whitespace. Make the visual feel hand-crafted, expensive and technically credible. Tiny typography, structured micro-detail, excellent rhythm, aligned financial values, crisp line icons, well-composed information density. Use a modern grotesk sans with tabular/monospace figures. Main headings 22–26 px, price 38–42 px, small detailed labels. Cards have modest 10 px radii, controlled subtle depth and 1 px fine borders. Dense data is beautifully organized.
